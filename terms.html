Terms of Use for Line Lab

Last updated: October 26, 2026

What Line Lab is

Line Lab is an entertainment and education app. It shows a statistical model's projected margins and totals alongside market lines and final scores, and it scores its own past projections honestly — including its misses.

Not betting advice

Nothing in Line Lab is betting advice, a tip, or a recommendation to wager. All projections are paper signals for calibration and discussion only. Past model performance does not predict future results, and no outcome is guaranteed. If you choose to wager anywhere else, you do so at your own risk and under that service's own terms and local laws.

No real-money wagering in the app

Line Lab takes no bets, holds no money, and offers no prizes, payouts, or cash-outs. Any bet records shown in the app are your own private history, stored on your device.

Age requirement

You must be 18 or older to use Line Lab.

Data accuracy

Scores, lines, and projections come from public sources and may be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. Line Lab is provided "as is," without warranties of any kind. Always verify a line or score with the source before acting on it.

Responsible play

If gambling stops being fun, help is available 24/7 in the U.S. at 1-800-GAMBLER. You can also contact the National Council on Problem Gambling at ncpgambling.org.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the app, attempt to disrupt its data sources, or present its output as your own professional advice service.

Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time; the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
