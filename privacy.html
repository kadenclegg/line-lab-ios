Privacy Policy for Line Lab

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Line Lab is a sports-model companion app. It shows model-projected margins and totals next to market lines and final scores, for entertainment and education. It does not take bets, hold money, or facilitate real-money wagering.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Line Lab requires no account, no sign-in, and no personal details to use. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or device identifiers, and we do not sell or share any data because there is none to share.

Information stored on your device only

Your preferences — such as favorite teams and which sports you hide — are stored locally on your device and never leave it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Network requests

The app fetches publicly available sports data (schedules, scores, market lines, and team logos) from public sources such as ESPN over secure connections. These requests do not include any personal information about you. Team logos are loaded from ESPN's public image servers.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, they are delivered through Apple's push service. We do not associate push tokens with any identity, and you can turn notifications off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Analytics and advertising

Line Lab contains no third-party analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking.

Children

Line Lab is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact
